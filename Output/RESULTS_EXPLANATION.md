# Variable Annuity Analysis Results Explanation

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive explanation of the results generated by the Variable Annuity risk analysis, focusing on the effects of different stochastic volatility models and dynamic hedging strategies on capital requirements.

---

## Key Results Files

### 1. comprehensive_hedging_analysis.csv

**Purpose**: Complete risk metrics comparison across all models and hedging strategies

**Column Definitions**:
- **Model**: Stochastic volatility model used (GBM, Heston, RoughVol)
- **Strategy**: Hedging approach (Unhedged, Daily, Weekly, Monthly)
- **Mean_PnL**: Expected profit/loss per $1,000 contract from insurer perspective
- **Std_PnL**: Standard deviation of profit/loss (volatility measure)
- **VaR_95**: Value-at-Risk at 95% confidence level (5% worst-case threshold)
- **VaR_99**: Value-at-Risk at 99% confidence level (1% worst-case threshold) 
- **CTE_95**: Conditional Tail Expectation at 95% (expected loss beyond VaR_95)
- **Worst_Case**: Single worst outcome across all simulations
- **Prob_Loss**: Probability of experiencing a loss (negative PnL)

**Key Insights**:
- **Unhedged GBM**: Mean loss of $109.86, moderate risk (Std: $689.43)
- **Unhedged Heston**: Larger mean loss of $169.11, high risk (Std: $1,583.53)
- **Unhedged RoughVol**: Positive mean return of $40.99, but high volatility
- **Hedging Benefits**: All models show substantial improvement with hedging
- **GBM with Daily Hedging**: Transforms losses into $731.71 mean profit, 0% loss probability
- **Heston with Daily Hedging**: $327.98 mean profit, significant risk reduction
- **RoughVol with Daily Hedging**: $399.36 mean profit, substantial volatility reduction

### 2. hedging_summary_table.csv

**Purpose**: Detailed breakdown including Solvency Capital Requirement (SCR) estimates

**Column Definitions**:
- **Model**: Stochastic volatility model
- **Strategy**: Hedging frequency or unhedged
- **Mean P&L**: Expected profit/loss per contract
- **Std P&L**: Standard deviation of outcomes
- **95% VaR**: 95% Value-at-Risk
- **99% VaR**: 99% Value-at-Risk
- **95% CTE**: 95% Conditional Tail Expectation
- **SCR (99.5% CTE)**: Solvency Capital Requirement estimate (regulatory capital proxy)
- **Prob(Loss)**: Probability of loss scenarios

**Key Capital Requirements Findings**:
- **GBM Model**: Requires $540 unhedged capital, completely eliminated with hedging
- **Heston Model**: Requires $2,250 unhedged capital, reduced to ~$500 with hedging
- **RoughVol Model**: Similar high unhedged requirements, substantial hedging benefits
- **Capital Relief**: Hedging provides $688-$1,774 capital relief per contract

## Model Comparison Summary

### Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM)
- **Risk Profile**: Moderate, predictable losses
- **Unhedged Performance**: -$109.86 mean, $689.43 volatility
- **Hedging Effectiveness**: Exceptional (near-perfect)
- **Capital Requirements**: Low ($540 unhedged)
- **Best Use**: Baseline comparison, simple implementations

### Heston Stochastic Volatility
- **Risk Profile**: Higher losses, fat-tailed distributions
- **Unhedged Performance**: -$169.11 mean, $1,583.53 volatility  
- **Hedging Effectiveness**: Good but imperfect
- **Capital Requirements**: High ($2,250 unhedged)
- **Best Use**: Realistic market modeling, sophisticated risk management

### Rough Volatility
- **Risk Profile**: Positive expected returns but extreme volatility
- **Unhedged Performance**: +$40.99 mean, $1,305.42 volatility
- **Hedging Effectiveness**: Meaningful but challenging
- **Capital Requirements**: High ($2,250 unhedged)
- **Best Use**: Cutting-edge research, highly sophisticated modeling

## Hedging Strategy Analysis

### Daily Rebalancing
- **Advantages**: Most responsive to market changes, best risk reduction
- **Disadvantages**: Highest transaction costs
- **Performance**: Superior across all models
- **Recommended For**: Large portfolios with sophisticated operations

### Weekly Rebalancing
- **Advantages**: Good balance of effectiveness and costs
- **Disadvantages**: Some hedge slippage during volatile periods
- **Performance**: Nearly as good as daily, lower costs
- **Recommended For**: Most practical implementations

### Monthly Rebalancing
- **Advantages**: Lowest transaction costs, operational simplicity
- **Disadvantages**: Significant hedge slippage risk
- **Performance**: Basic risk reduction
- **Recommended For**: Small portfolios or limited operational capability

## Capital Requirements Analysis

### Regulatory Capital Impact

**Unhedged Capital Requirements (per $1,000 contract)**:
- GBM Model: $540
- Heston Model: $2,250 (4x higher than GBM)
- Rough Volatility Model: $2,250 (4x higher than GBM)

**Hedged Capital Requirements (Daily hedging)**:
- GBM Model: -$147.59 (capital surplus)
- Heston Model: $530.36 (76% reduction)
- Rough Volatility Model: $669.60 (70% reduction)

**Capital Relief from Hedging**:
- GBM: $687.59 per contract (127% of unhedged requirement)
- Heston: $1,719.64 per contract (76% of unhedged requirement)
- Rough Vol: $1,580.40 per contract (70% of unhedged requirement)

### Economic Justification

**Return on Hedging Investment**:
- Typical hedging costs: 2-4% of notional value
- Capital relief: $688-$1,774 per $1,000 contract
- Return ratio: 20:1 to 50:1 across models
- **Conclusion**: Hedging is economically justified across all models
